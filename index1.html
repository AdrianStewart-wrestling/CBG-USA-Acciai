Meat processing division

Four brands, one CBG facility.

CBG's meat-processing blades are produced under four brands built specifically for butcher and processing-plant equipment, with roughly 40 years of manufacturing history behind them.

Salvador

Butcher band-saw blades — welded or in coils.

Salvinox

Stainless mincer & grinder plates and knives.

Tornado

Bone-saw ring blades for fresh, bone-in cuts.

DorBlade

Additional band-saw & ring-blade profiles.

Buy genuine. Salvador and Salvinox products have been counterfeited in some markets. CBG USA is the authorized channel for genuine product, backed by manufacturer warranty and support.

About CBG Acciai

Nearly a century of blade manufacturing.

Headquartered in Caronno Pertusella, near Milan, CBG Acciai manufactures high-precision blades from Swedish steel, distributed through a global network of specialized agents and distributors.

The company was majority acquired in 2022 by Azimut Libera Impresa and HLD, with the Filippini Fantoni family retaining a stake and remaining involved. HLD has positioned CBG as a buy-and-build platform in a niche market growing an estimated 5–7% per year.
