Narrow Blades

Commercial shop and detail-curve profiles from 6mm to 50mm wide, ground in three steel grades to match crack resistance against fatigue life.

Overview

Narrow blades are the format for commercial cabinet shops, resaws, and detail-curve work where maneuverability matters more than raw throughput. C.B.G. grinds narrow stock in three steel grades so a shop can weigh cut life against resistance to cracking on tight-radius cuts.
