Mincer System Architectures Supported

1. Enterprise Cutting Systems (Single Cut Format)

The universal configuration running across typical supermarket display counters and light processing hubs. Combines a single four-blade cross-knife rotating flush against one reversible or hubbed grinding disc.

2. Unger Multi-Stage Cutting Systems (Partial / Total Formats)

Engineered for large industrial volume mincing networks. Utilizes specialized ring knives outfitted with precision interchangeable/replaceable blades. This lets high-throughput meat packagers switch out dull cutting facets rapidly without throwing away structural ring chassis units.

Salvinox components are machined to exact industry dimensions matching universal grinder hub designations—including #5, #8, #12, #22, and #32 standards. To stop premature dragging or uneven wear trails, knives and plates should always be replaced as matching synchronized pairs.
